**Result type:** `completion_certificate`, an official, verifiable freeCodeCamp-issued certification. This classification does not imply an accredited academic diploma, government license, or independent industry certification.

**Language caveat:** the issuer's December 2025 FAQ states that its certification exams were in English only at that time. Because the complete set of learning languages was not verified on the current course UI, the structured record leaves `languages` empty instead of guessing.

**Duration caveat:** older provider plans estimated roughly 300 hours of curriculum, but actual course duration was not independently confirmed from the current v9 course page. The record uses `duration_hours: null`.

**Exam rules:** the official release describes a minimum seven-day interval between attempts, a closed-book exam, and staff moderation. Learners must not use AI tools, outside notes or other people during the actual exam. These rules do not prohibit using research tools while choosing or studying a course.

**Individual verification:** issuer support documents a per-learner certification URL. No learner-specific credential was issued or independently inspected for this research, so the course record does not fabricate an `individual_verification_url`.
