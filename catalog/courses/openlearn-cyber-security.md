## Официальные результаты и условия

### Open University Statement of Participation — Introduction to cyber security: stay safe online

**Тип:** подтверждение участия · **Стоимость получения:** бесплатно

Sign in and enrol; read Introduction and Guidance and every course page; submit the quizzes. Download the free statement from My OpenLearn after completion; notification can take 24 hours. No formal qualification credit.

[Условия награды на официальном сайте](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/digital-computing/introduction-cyber-security-stay-safe-online/content-section-overview)

### Open University digital badge — Introduction to cyber security: stay safe online

**Тип:** цифровой бейдж · **Стоимость получения:** бесплатно

Enrol and sign in, read each of the eight weeks, and score at least 50% in both badge quizzes in Weeks 4 and 8. Most questions allow three attempts, true/false usually one; later correct attempts earn fewer marks, so answering all questions eventually correctly does not itself guarantee a passing score. Badge is not an accredited OU qualification. After a failed badge quiz, the whole quiz can be retaken after 24 hours, with no stated limit on whole-quiz retakes.

[Условия награды на официальном сайте](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/mod/oucontent/view.php?id=50885&section=1.2)

### 24 CPD points — Introduction to cyber security: stay safe online

**Тип:** баллы CPD / CEU · **Стоимость получения:** бесплатно

Successfully complete the named OpenLearn course while enrolled. The Open University states that 24 continuing professional development (CPD) points are awarded on successful completion; evidence appears on the course's free Statement of Participation. These are CPD points, not transferable academic credits or a separate degree. No separately purchased CPD credential is described.

[Условия награды на официальном сайте](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/digital-computing/introduction-cyber-security-stay-safe-online/content-section-overview)
