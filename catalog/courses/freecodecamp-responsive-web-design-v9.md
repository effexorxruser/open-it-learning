## Официальные результаты и условия

### Responsive Web Design Certification (freeCodeCamp, v9)

**Тип:** сертификат об окончании · **Стоимость получения:** бесплатно

Complete the five certification projects, meeting their automated project tests, to qualify for the 50-question exam. Accept the freeCodeCamp academic honesty pledge, take and pass the freeCodeCamp Exam Environment desktop exam, then await staff moderation of results. After a passing result, claim the certificate in account settings. The issuer's published FAQ describes English-only exams and a minimum 168-hour interval between attempts; recheck these details if they change.

[Условия награды на официальном сайте](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/freecodecamps-new-responsive-web-design-certification-is-now-live)
