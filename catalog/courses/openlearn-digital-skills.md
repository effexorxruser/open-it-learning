## Официальные результаты и условия

### Open University Statement of Participation — Digital skills: succeeding in a digital world

**Тип:** подтверждение участия · **Стоимость получения:** бесплатно

Sign in and enrol; read Introduction and Guidance and every course page; submit the quizzes. Download the free statement from My OpenLearn after completion; notification can take 24 hours. No formal qualification credit.

[Условия награды на официальном сайте](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/digital-computing/digital-skills-succeeding-digital-world/content-section-overview)

### Open University digital badge — Digital skills: succeeding in a digital world

**Тип:** цифровой бейдж · **Стоимость получения:** бесплатно

Sign in and enrol, read all eight weeks and score at least 50% on each of the two badge quizzes in Weeks 4 and 8. Most quiz questions allow up to three attempts, true/false questions usually one; later correct responses score fewer marks. If an entire badge quiz is failed, it can be retaken after 24 hours, with no stated limit on whole-quiz retakes. A badge is not an accredited OU degree or professional license.

[Условия награды на официальном сайте](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/mod/oucontent/view.php?id=108797&section=_unit1.1.2)

### 24 CPD points — Digital skills: succeeding in a digital world

**Тип:** баллы CPD / CEU · **Стоимость получения:** бесплатно

Successfully complete the named OpenLearn course while enrolled. The Open University states that 24 continuing professional development (CPD) points are awarded on successful completion; evidence appears on the course's free Statement of Participation. These are CPD points, not transferable academic credits or a separate degree. No separately purchased CPD credential is described.

[Условия награды на официальном сайте](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/digital-computing/digital-skills-succeeding-digital-world/content-section-overview)
