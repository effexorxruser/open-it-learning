## Important limits

- **CS50:** The free **CS50 Certificate** is not the purchased verified certificate from edX or academic credit. The current issuer FAQ lists an **overall assignment submission deadline of 30 June 2027 at 23:59 UTC**; recheck before planning to finish. The issuer also advises downloading the free certificate before that deadline. An already issued certificate is not claimed to expire on that date.
- **OpenLearn:** A Statement of Participation, a digital badge and **24 continuing professional development (CPD) points** are separate *types of results* for the two badged courses. The CPD evidence is included on the free statement, **not** a separate university degree, transferable academic credit or professional license. Both badge courses require all eight weeks and at least 50% on **both** Week 4 and Week 8 badge quizzes; the whole quiz can be retried after 24 hours following failure.
- **freeCodeCamp:** The current Python and Responsive Web Design certifications require projects, a separate exam and staff moderation. Do not confuse them with retired legacy curricula or assume instant issuance.
- **Saylor CS101/CS302:** The ordinary 70%+ final exam and completion certificate are free. CS101 uses a 14-day wait between retakes and at most three tries; CS302's ordinary certificate exam uses a seven-day wait. CS302's optional ACE-credit proctored exam costs $5 and is not necessary for a free course certificate. Their CEUs appear on the free completion documents, not as separate academic degrees or guaranteed transferable credit.
- **Stepik:** The dated official API thresholds are **100/113** (Linux), **136/144** (SQL), and **90/106** (C++), respectively regular/distinction; the courses and their certificate feature are currently free. Requirements can change, so recheck the API.
- **IBM / Microsoft:** IBM AI Literacy awards an IBM SkillsBuild *digital badge*, while Microsoft Learn awards a *module achievement badge*. Neither is represented as a paid professional exam qualification.
- **1С «Код будущего»:** Its [September 2026 course announcement](https://x.1c-uc3.ru/about/news/besplatnoe-obuchenie-programmirovaniyu-dlya-shkolnikov-v-ramkakh-proekta-kod-budushchego/) documents restricted free in-person Moscow training and module certificates, but not the fee for issuing the certificate. It remains [under review](../../journal/research-batch-002/cards/one-c-future-code-element-2026.json) and is **not** among the 21 verified courses.
- **Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity** remains [under review](../../journal/2026-10-28-cisco-introduction-to-cybersecurity.md) because its exact badge issuance conditions were not adequately established.
