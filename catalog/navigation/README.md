### Quality and publication boundary

Only existing `catalog/verified/*.json` records are listed. The [1С / Код будущего candidate](../../journal/research-batch-002/cards/one-c-future-code-element-2026.json) belongs conceptually to **ERP and 1С**, but is **not listed as verified** because the certificate issuance fee remains unknown. Unverified candidates stay in the journal.

Labels are available in **EN/RU** in taxonomy v1; README's Ukrainian and Chinese navigation links lead to the same canonical pages while full taxonomy translations remain future work. A new topic, course or specialization must be registered and validated before publishing navigation changes.
